## Example recovery process

1. Check service health to understand what is affected.
2. Review the last deployment for changes that could explain the symptoms.
3. Confirm recovery with the service owner before closing the incident.

This example contains no real infrastructure commands, customer information or production endpoints. In a real integration, the connector would read the source runbook and preserve its access permissions.
